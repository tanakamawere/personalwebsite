## A home for the things I build

Alcanzar is my software company. It brings together my work on applications for auctions, clinics, marketplaces, and schools.

### Under the Alcanzar umbrella

- **Auction App** — an online platform for buying and selling through auctions.
- **Clinic Management** — software for patient care, records, and day-to-day clinic administration.
- **Marketplace** — an e-commerce application connecting buyers and sellers.
- **School App** — management software for students, teachers, and administrators.
