## About School App

School App is a school management platform built to simplify how academic institutions handle daily operations. It brings students, teachers, parents, and administrators into one connected system for communication, record keeping, and academic management.

### Key Features

- **Student Records**: Maintain organized student profiles, enrollment details, and academic history
- **Class Management**: Manage classes, timetables, and subject assignments in one place
- **Results & Reports**: Capture marks, generate report cards, and review performance trends
- **Attendance Tracking**: Record and monitor student attendance efficiently
- **Parent Communication**: Keep parents informed with updates, notices, and academic feedback
- **Staff Administration**: Support teacher management and administrative workflows
- **Responsive Access**: Designed to work across desktop and mobile devices

### Who It Helps

1. **Administrators**: Oversee school operations, student records, and reporting
2. **Teachers**: Manage classes, attendance, and assessment results
3. **Parents & Students**: Stay updated on performance, announcements, and school information

### Technology & Value

The platform is designed to reduce paperwork, improve visibility across school processes, and make academic administration more efficient. By centralizing important workflows, School App helps institutions save time and communicate more effectively.
